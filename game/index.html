    How to play
    
      
    
        
    1 · Read the dossier
    Intel arrives line by line. The first line is free; the rest drop every few seconds. Tap a locked line to buy it now for 8 seconds.
    
        
    2 · Watch the crowd
    Drag to look around. Use the lens to zoom, but keep it short: hold it too long and your glint warns the courier.
    
        
    3 · Provoke
    Noise makes everyone stop and look, except a trained courier and anyone wearing headphones. Ping hits the cell radio: every Red Signal member touches their ear.
    
        
    4 · Tag
    Tap a person to select them. Hold TAG until the ring locks, then release. Wait for a clear line: passers-by block the shot.
    
        
    5 · Be brave
    Tagging early, before all the intel arrives, multiplies your score. A wrong tag spooks the courier: you have one more shot and 15 seconds.
    
        
    Echo lens
    From case 4: replay the last 8 seconds of the crowd, once per case, for 6 seconds of your clock. Use it to catch who didn't flinch at the noise or who touched an ear. Drag and zoom while it plays.
    
        
    Daily Case
    One case a day, identical for every player. Share your result without spoilers.
